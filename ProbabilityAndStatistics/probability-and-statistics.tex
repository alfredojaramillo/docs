\begin{abstract}
Notes developing the likelihood function from the binomial example: the shape of
$\like(q \given v, n)$, the sense in which the likelihood ``is'' the probability
mass function with argument and parameter exchanged, and the measure-theoretic
definition together with the three qualifications it carries.
\end{abstract}
